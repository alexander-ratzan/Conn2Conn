## #6 · Equivalence (TOST) and minimum detectable effect

Practical-equivalence margin δ = ±0.02 pearson. A cell is 'equivalent to zero' if its lift is statistically inside [−δ, +δ] (TOST, α=0.05).

- Median MDE at 80% power across cells: **0.056** pearson (i.e. lifts smaller than this could not be reliably detected with n=10 seeds; the design is well-powered for the ~0.1 lifts of interest but not for sub-0.03 effects).

- Of the `obs_SC`/`pred_SC` cells, 0/12 are statistically equivalent to zero; the remainder sit **below** the band (genuinely negative, not merely non-significant). Either way none are positive.
