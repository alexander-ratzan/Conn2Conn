# Cross-model benchmark (spec v2 E2.2: MSE-only, both directions)

**Status:** complete 2026-10-04 (SC → FC and FC → SC, seeds 0–4) · **Owner:** agent:modeling ·
**Spec:** `context_packages/repo_spec_docs/spec_doc_v2.md` E2.2 (closed); follow-ups carried to `spec_doc_v3.md`

## Question

With every model tuned on the same splits under MSE only, how are test Pearson r, demeaned r, average rank and top-1
accuracy distributed across model types, in each direction? The ranking picks the models for the composite-loss
benchmark with tuned weights (v2 E2.3, carried to v3). The FC → SC half is spec v2 E3's "replicate E2 for FC → SC".

## Design

- **Splits / selection:** `shuffle_seed` 0–4 (family-preserving splits; seed 0 = the original split). Each model is
  tuned per seed and selected on **val demeaned r**; test metrics come from the best trial's report
  (`--report_best_after_tune`). Never selected on test.
- **Loss:** MSE only (`composite` with one `mse` term). Loss-weight and EMA keys are removed from every search;
  Sarwar's correlation term is off. Two models enter with their native objective, labelled `*` in every figure:
  `MaskedMLPPretrainer` (masked latent reconstruction, `latent_mse`) and Krakencoder (see Reused results).
- **One campaign per model × direction:** job name `e2_mse_<Model>_<direction>`; results come only from that
  campaign's task logs, never the best over older sweeps (which carry v2:C!1 / v2:C!4).
- **Own batch size and budget per model** (D4's batch 64 is a composite-loss rule).
- **Trial budget:** `clamp(8 × free keys, 16, 64)` with Optuna + ASHA; closed-form grid models run their full grid;
  the audit-narrowed models run the narrowed searches below.

Narrowed searches (from the 2026-09-30 audit of 3,592 past trials, where extra trials bought less than seed noise):
Chen (identity nodes, 2 layers, 500 epochs; `conv_dim`, `dnn_dim`, `lr`, `l2_reg`), Nodal GNN (2 layers, decoder 32,
500 epochs; `hidden_dim`, `lr`, `l2_reg`), Sarwar (leaky ReLU, 300 epochs; layers, hidden, dropout, `lr`, `l2_reg`),
Nodal MLP (three keys from E0's importance table), Masked MLP (k = 128, nonlinear, MLP readout; masks, hidden,
dropout, `l2_reg`, `lr`, epochs). Generated by [`build_configs.py`](build_configs.py) into
`models/configs/benchmark/mse/` (direction-agnostic; `Sarwar2020MLP_fc2sc.yml` sets `output_tanh: false` because SC
targets reach 3.59).

**Rosters.** FC → SC excludes Chen GCN, Nodal GNN and Nodal MLP: their reverse variants are not built (spec v2 E2.0;
SC-derived inputs would leak the target, and negative FC weights break GCN normalisation).

**Latent gate (D2).** Seeds 0–1, 12 trials; the Masked MLP enters the full run if its mean val demeaned r ≥ the
MSE-only PCA-PLS learnable on the same seeds − 0.01.

| Direction | Masked MLP val | Learnable val | Threshold | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SC → FC | 0.088 | 0.096 | 0.086 | passed → full run |
| FC → SC | 0.132 | 0.179 | 0.169 | **failed** → negative result, not in the FC → SC figures (`fc2sc/tables/excluded.md`) |

**Reused results** (no rerun):
- **Krakencoder** (spec v2 E2.1 retrain, seeds 0–4, both directions), two variants (user 2026-10-02):
  - *Krakencoder (MSE)* = its `mse_only` cell: MSE plus Krakencoder's fixed latent losses (no output identity terms);
  - *Krakencoder (paper loss)* = `kraken_default`: adds its native corr-eye and neighbor-distance terms.
  Both are labelled `*` (native objective). Records: `composite_loss/krakencoder/<direction>/tables/seed_records.csv`.
- **Test-retest ceiling** (spec v2 E1.10; SC → FC only, no SC retest in the data): `composite_loss/ceiling/test_retest.csv`.

**Execution.** Pilot (seed 0 per model, gate seeds 0–1) → budgets from pilot wall times → full runs, driven by
[`autopilot.py`](autopilot.py) (time limit = 1.5 × pilot wall per seed, retries ×2, 130 GPU-h cap, stop on > 20 %
errored trials or a second failure). **Compute: 111.5 GPU-h** (SC → FC 80.4, FC → SC 31.1, pilots included).
Four tasks (Sarwar SC → FC seed 1 and FC → SC seeds 3–4, Nodal MLP SC → FC seed 4) were killed by the cluster
about 2 h 10–25 min in at 0.5 GPU × 2 trials, consistent with its GPU-underutilisation policy; both models were
repacked to 0.25 × 4 (same trial budget) and the seeds rerun once, successfully. Nodal MLP seed 4's rerun lost 3 of
12 trials at iteration 0 (all batch 32 / embedding 64; likely GPU memory at the tighter packing; the Ray error files
were on node-local `/tmp`); its best trial comes from the other 9.

### Findings

1. **SC → FC: the linear family is on top and statistically tied.** Linear backbone, PCA-PLS + covariates,
   Conditional Gaussian and PCA-PLS are within 1 SE of each other (demeaned r 0.092–0.098). No nonlinear model beats
   them: the Masked MLP (0.090) and Krakencoder (MSE, 0.086) are close behind, Sarwar is 0.03 lower, and Chen GCN and
   both nodal models are at the null, confirming E0 with per-seed tuning.
2. **Identifiability and edge fidelity rank models differently.** Krakencoder's paper loss has the highest SC → FC
   avg rank (0.802) and top-1 (0.067) at a demeaned-r cost (0.080 vs 0.086 for its MSE cell), the same trade-off as
   E1. Among MSE-only models, Conditional Gaussian and the linear backbone have the best avg rank (0.744).
3. **FC → SC is easier for every model** (demeaned r ×1.4–1.9 per model; avg rank 0.86–0.90 vs 0.64–0.80; the
   null is about 0.01 in both directions), matching spec v2 E3 Phase D. PCA-PLS learnable and the linear backbone lead the input-matched models;
   Krakencoder's paper loss costs demeaned r with no avg-rank gain, as in E1.8.
4. **The latent pretrainer helps in neither direction.** It ties the linear family SC → FC and fails the FC → SC
   gate by 0.04.
5. **Far from the ceiling.** The best SC → FC model recovers about 20 % of the test-retest demeaned r (0.098 vs
   0.49) and 7 % of its top-1.

**Carried forward (composite benchmark with tuned weights, v3):** SC → FC — linear backbone, Conditional Gaussian,
PCA-PLS learnable (gradient-trained, so composite terms apply), with Krakencoder as the deep reference; FC → SC —
PCA-PLS learnable and the linear backbone. Closed-form PCA-PLS cannot take composite terms and stays as the reference.

## Caveats

- **PCA-PLS + covariates is not input-matched (†).** It sees FreeSurfer `fs_all` and demographics besides the source
  connectome. FC → SC its lead (top-1 0.45 vs 0.13) most likely comes from `fs_all` regional volumes predicting the
  SC target's `invnodevol` normalisation (streamline counts ÷ node volume), i.e. anatomy rather than FC (spec v2 E3
  Phase D found the same). It is never the paired reference; a no-volume / demographics-only ablation is open (v3).
- **Native objectives.** `*` rows are not MSE-only; they are reported for reference, labelled in every table and figure.
- **Closed-form grid rows** include infeasible grid points (PCA-PLS: 10 % errored, PLS components > PCA components);
  they do not affect the best trial.
- **Reused Krakencoder rows** come from the E2.1 retrain on the same splits, trained at batch 64 (the composite-loss
  protocol), not tuned per seed.

## Layout

```
models/configs/benchmark/mse/<Model>.yml        # one MSE-only benchmark config per model (both directions)
models/configs/benchmark/mse/<Model>_fc2sc.yml  # only where FC -> SC needs a different model setting (Sarwar)
scripts/experiments/model_benchmark/
├── build_configs.py      # generates the configs above from the model defaults + declared narrowing (SPECS)
├── config.yml            # roster per direction, model types (figure groups), packing, reused results, gate, budget
├── submit.py             # submits one SLURM array per (model, direction): job name e2_mse_<Model>_<direction>
├── autopilot.py          # status / gate / budget / launch from the campaign's task logs (host python: needs squeue)
├── launch_model.sh       # one seed per array task: Tune + best-trial report
├── run.py                # task logs -> records.json -> tables + figures, per direction
├── checks/check_benchmark.py
├── audit/                # 2026-09-30 tuning-history audit (collect_trials.py, analyze_search_spaces.py)
└── mse/                  # E2.2 results (tracked); the composite benchmark adds composite/, the final one final/
    ├── sc2fc/            #   records.json, tables/, figures/
    └── fc2sc/
```

## How to run

| Step | Command |
|---|---|
| (Re)generate configs | `python scripts/experiments/model_benchmark/build_configs.py` (`--check` to verify) |
| Pilot (1 seed per model + latent gate on seeds 0–1) | `python scripts/experiments/model_benchmark/submit.py --direction sc2fc --stage pilot` |
| Status, gate, budget (host) | `python3 scripts/experiments/model_benchmark/autopilot.py {status,gate,budget} --direction sc2fc` |
| Full run (host; only missing seeds, time from pilot) | `python3 scripts/experiments/model_benchmark/autopilot.py launch --direction sc2fc [--dry-run]` |
| Collect + figures (kraken env) | `python scripts/experiments/model_benchmark/run.py --direction sc2fc` (`--cached` re-renders) |
| Checks (CPU) | `python scripts/experiments/model_benchmark/checks/check_benchmark.py` |

`submit.py --stage full` still submits all seeds directly; `autopilot.py launch` is the budget-guarded path used here.

## Figures (`mse/<direction>/figures/`)

- `bars_<metric>.png` for Pearson r, demeaned r, average rank and top-1, and `bars_all_metrics.png` (2 × 2): bars
  grouped and coloured by model type, groups sorted by their mean and models by their own mean; mean ± SE with
  per-seed points; native-objective models hatched (`*`), extra-input models marked †; PCA null as a dotted line;
  test-retest ceiling as a dashed line, or noted in the title when off scale; footnote lists models not shown.
- `bars_all_metrics_ceiling.png` (SC → FC): the same panel with the ceiling drawn as its own bar.
- `scatter_demeaned_vs_rank.png`: one point per model (mean ± SE), demeaned r against average rank.
